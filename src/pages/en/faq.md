## The leaderboard

**Why do I only see myself on the leaderboard when more people have walked?**

The walk has been split into several rounds. A round opens when someone starts
the walk, and everyone who begins while it is running shares one leaderboard.
Once more than eight hours have passed without anyone answering a question, the
round counts as finished and the next person to start gets a new one.

That is meant for anyone running the same walk week after week who does not
want last week's names on the list. But if your walk stays up for several days
and people go whenever they can, each day becomes its own round — and everyone
sees only themselves.

The fix is to turn the walk into an **event with dates**, see the next question.
Nothing is lost in the meantime: earlier results stay and reappear on the shared
list once the dates are set.

**How do I get one leaderboard for a whole week?**

Turn on event mode:

1. **Library → My walks**
2. Tap the pencil to edit the walk
3. Expand **Settings** and turn on **Event with dates**
4. Set a start and an end date
5. **Save**

Everyone who walks during that period now lands on the same leaderboard,
whatever day they went. Running the walk again later? Just set new dates and
the list starts over.

**Is the leaderboard still there after the event ends?**

Yes. Open the walk as usual and it says the event has ended, with a **Show
leaderboard** button that takes you to the results. The list stays up until you
change the event dates, which starts a new round.

**Can I see who took part?**

The leaderboard is that list. Everyone who finished is shown with name, score
and percentage. Anyone still walking appears further down in grey, with a bar
showing how far they have got. As the creator you reach it through
**Library → My walks → ⋯ → Leaderboard**.

To see how the questions went, **Insights** sits in the same menu: participants,
average score, average step count and how the answers were spread per question.
Insights counts every round together, so its number can be higher than the names
on a single leaderboard.

**How do I end a round early?**

**Library → My walks → ⋯ → End round**, or the button at the bottom of the
leaderboard. The round locks and the next person to start begins a new one with
an empty list. It cannot be undone, so anyone mid-round can no longer save
answers — the app warns you and says how many that affects before you confirm.

You rarely need to do this yourself: a round that has been idle for eight hours
is cleaned up automatically.

**Can I run the same walk again next week?**

Yes, there is no need to create a new one. The next time someone starts, a new
round opens with an empty leaderboard. With event mode you set new dates instead.

## During the walk

**The question will not open even though I am standing at the checkpoint.**

GPS unlocks the question within about 15 metres, or 50 metres if the creator
chose bike mode. Check that location services are on for the app and that you
are outdoors — between tall buildings GPS can be tens of metres off. The phone
vibrates when a checkpoint triggers, so you can keep it in your pocket.

If the creator enabled enforced order, checkpoints must be taken in sequence.
The others show a padlock on the map until the previous one is answered.

**Can I pause and continue later?**

Yes. Reopen the walk on the same phone and it tells you how many questions you
answered, offering **Continue walk** or **Start over**. This works even after a
long break. Switching phones, or reinstalling without being signed in, means
starting over.

**Does the app work without coverage?**

Yes, once the walk has loaded. Questions and map tiles are stored on the phone
and answers are sent when you are back online. You do need internet the first
time you open a walk.

## For creators

**The correct answer is always first — can I shuffle the options?**

They are shuffled automatically now when you take questions from a question
pack. To reshuffle a walk you already made, use **Shuffle answer options** above
the question list while editing. Options that are years or numbers are sorted in
ascending order instead, which is what reads naturally there.

**Can I keep the results hidden until the prize ceremony?**

Yes. Turn on **Hidden results until you reveal** under Settings when creating or
editing the walk. Participants then see no right or wrong, no score and no
leaderboard until you tap **Reveal results** on the leaderboard.

**How do I share the walk?**

The share button gives you a link that opens the walk straight in the app, plus
a QR code to print. The same link works for everyone — it is what decides which
leaderboard you end up on.

## Account and data

**Do I need an account to take part?**

No, you only enter a name. An account is needed only to create your own walks.

**What does it cost?**

Creating and walking is free, with no ads. The only paid part is AI-generated
questions, which is an optional extra.

**How do I delete my account and my data?**

**Settings → Danger zone** in the app, where you type RADERA to confirm. If you
no longer have the app, there are instructions at
**[/radera-konto](/radera-konto)**. What is stored and for how long is covered in
the **[privacy policy](/integritet)**.

**Which languages does the app support?**

Swedish, English, German, Norwegian, Danish, Finnish, French and Spanish. Pick
one under **Settings → Language**, or let the app follow your phone.
