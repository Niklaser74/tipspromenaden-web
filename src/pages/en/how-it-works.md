## For participants — you got a QR code or a link

1. **Open the link or scan the QR code.** If the app is installed, the
   walk opens directly. Otherwise it opens in your browser first, where
   you can choose to install the app and continue.
2. **Enter your name.** It shows up on the leaderboard for everyone with
   the same link.
3. **Tap Start.** The walk opens on a map with numbered control points.
4. **Walk to the first control.** The question unlocks automatically
   when GPS confirms you are there — the app gives a soft vibration so
   you know it triggered.
5. **Answer the question and move on.** Once you have done all
   controls, you see your score and land on the leaderboard.

**Tip:** GPS needs to be on, and you need an internet connection to
load the walk the first time. After that it works offline if you lose
signal outside.

## For creators — you want to build your own walk

### The quick way (in the app)

1. Tap **Create walk** on the home screen (requires Google sign-in).
2. **Tap the map** where each control should be. You can also import a
   ready-made question pack from [/en/tipspack](/en/tipspack) — then you
   just place the questions on the map.
3. **Write the question and the answer options** for each control. Mark
   the correct answer.
4. **Save.** You get a QR code you can share directly or print. Send
   the code or the link to the participants.

### The roomy way (on the web)

Prefer a laptop and a real keyboard? Go to [**/skapa**](/skapa) and
build the walk there. Same result — your walk lands in your collection
in the app as soon as you sign in.

> The web creator interface is currently Swedish-only. The walk you
> build there works just like any other walk in the app.

### What you can configure

- **Images per question** — upload an image shown together with the question.
- **Activity type** — pick **🚶 Walk** or **🚲 Bike**. Bike mode uses a
  wider GPS trigger (50 m instead of 15 m), an "approaching" warning at
  100 m so the cyclist has time to slow down, and a wider default map zoom.
- **Category and city** — so others can find your walk if you publish it.
- **Event mode** — set start and end dates so everyone shares one common leaderboard.
- **Publish to library** — make the walk discoverable for everyone in the app's library.

## Finding ready-made walks

Tap **Library** in the app to browse published walks and ready
question packs. Toggle **"📍 Near me"** to sort with the nearest walk
first.

## FAQ

**Do I need to sign in to participate?**
No. You only need a name. Signing in is only needed to *create* your
own walks.

**What does it cost?**
Nothing. Tipspromenaden is a free hobby project with no ads and no
purchases. If you want to chip in to cover hosting costs you can
[support the project](/en/support) — entirely optional.

**Which languages are supported?**
The app is available in Swedish, English, German, Norwegian, Danish,
Finnish, French and Spanish. Pick one under **Settings → Language** or
let the app follow your system language. Questions you create can be
tagged with a language so the right people find them.

**Something is broken — who do I contact?**
Email **[tipspromenaden.app@gmail.com](mailto:tipspromenaden.app@gmail.com)**
and I will look into it.
